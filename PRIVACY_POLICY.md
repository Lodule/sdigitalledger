# Privacy Policy for Digital Ledger

**Effective Date:** March 30, 2025  
**Developer:** Lodule (`simonlodule18@gmail.com`)  
**Application:** Digital Ledger (com.sdigitalledger)

Digital Ledger ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the Digital Ledger Android application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Local Business & Ledger Data
- Inventory records, sale transactions, customer names, phone numbers, and debt balances entered by you are stored **locally on your device** using Room Database. We do not transmit or sell your raw business accounting ledger data to external servers.

### B. Usage & Analytics Data
- We collect anonymous usage events (e.g., screen views, feature clicks, performance metrics) via our Analytics Service to optimize user experience and app stability.

### C. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)
- The Free Version of Digital Ledger uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements.
- Google AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers (AAID) and device parameters to serve relevant ads. For more information, please review [Google's Advertising Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### D. Email Sequence & Feedback Submissions (Loops.so)
- When you submit feedback or opt into feature updates via the in-app Feedback Board, your email address (`simonlodule18@gmail.com` sequence dispatcher) and feedback rating are securely transmitted over HTTPS to trigger support follow-ups and automated onboarding sequences.

---

## 2. Data Security

We enforce enterprise-grade security standards to protect your information:
- **HTTPS & TLS 1.3/1.2**: All external communications (AdMob, Loops email triggers) require strict HTTPS encryption.
- **No Cleartext Traffic**: Disallowed in our Android Network Security Configuration.
- **Root & Tamper Detection**: Proactive checks to alert users if the runtime environment is compromised.
- **Data Backup Extraction Rules**: Configured to restrict unauthorized system-level ADB data extraction.

---

## 3. Data Retention & User Rights

- **Local Data Deletion**: You can erase all local sales and inventory data at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
- **Contact Us**: For privacy questions or data deletion requests regarding feedback emails, contact **Lodule** at `simonlodule18@gmail.com`.

---

## 4. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in this document and updated within the app.
